Decide which profile fields exist, their types, how they are keyed to upstream players, and which are public. The legacy fields are the starting point but not the limit.

## Open questions

- Which legacy fields carry over, which are dropped, which are new?
- Typed fields, flexible key/value attributes, or both?
- Which fields are personal data, and are they public by default?
- Are photos in scope, and are the old site's image files available?
